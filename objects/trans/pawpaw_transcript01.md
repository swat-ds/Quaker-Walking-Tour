7 Andromeda plumata. Plumed Andromeda, or Carolinian Ironwood Tree. (Bartram’s Catalogue)
This is also a southern beautiful Species of Andromeda; rising to the height of fifteen or twenty feet, and sending off towards the top, many spreading and nearly horizontal branches. The leaves are small, lance-shaped, and of a deep glossy green, but changing in Autumn before they fall off, to yellow, red, purple, &c. giving the trees a beautiful appearance, even in their decline. The flowers are produced at the extremity of the branches, in one-rowed racemes or bunches, they are very small and perfectly white, somewhat resembling a plume of delicate white feathers. This and the last mentioned, grow naturally by the sides of ponds, and swamps, in Carolina and Florida.
