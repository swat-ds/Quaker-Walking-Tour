## About Quaker Roots Collection

The Quaker Roots collection was started as a way to frame Swarthmore's beautiful campus within it's history. Specifically, the rich history with Quakers and their research with the different plants on Swarthmore Campus. 

This collection takes from Swarthmore Friends Library, the Biology Department's Bone Room, and various other sources to showcase the influence of Quakers on plants that can still lead students, vistors, admininstors throughout the campus to explore and learn.
